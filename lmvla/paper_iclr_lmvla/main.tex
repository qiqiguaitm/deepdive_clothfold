\subsubsection*{Reproducibility Statement}
The anonymized supplementary package includes preprocessing and evaluation
code, effective training configurations, checkpoint identifiers, and aggregate
evaluation reports.  Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix} records source and data hashes,
software versions, simulator seeds, and the currently available artifact
coverage.  The accepted-scene manifest, launch records, per-episode outcomes,
and paired three-seed report accompany the confirmatory matrix so that all
contrasts can be reproduced.  The oracle-transition follow-up additionally
includes three per-seed reports and its failed-gate decision JSON.  The
recurrence rollout diagnostic and semantic-subtask screen include artifact
audits, frozen protocols, per-episode outcomes, and rejected-gate records.  The
policy-preserving predictive branch additionally includes the five P1
seed-1000 reports, the accepted-gate JSON, both P2 replication reports, the
accepted three-seed gate, the matched efficiency report, the four R1 extension
reports, and the rejected R1 gate.  The outcome-calibrated branch includes all nine
three-arm reports, the accepted seed-1000 screen, and the rejected three-seed
replication gate.  Replicated claims are limited to branches whose frozen gate
completed; rejected branches remain reported as negative results.
