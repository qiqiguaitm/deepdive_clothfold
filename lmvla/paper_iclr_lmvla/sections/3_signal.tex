\section{Problem Setting}\label{sec:setup}
Let $h_t=E_\theta(o_t)$ be the policy representation, $y_t$ a future milestone
feature, and $q_t$ the condition presented to the action policy:
\begin{equation}
  a_t=\pi_\psi(h_t,q_t,l),
\end{equation}
where $l$ is the instruction.  A future-conditioned VLA couples four choices:
which future frame defines the target; whether its feature comes from an
external, frozen, or current encoder; whether the policy receives an absolute
feature or displacement; and whether the auxiliary gradient updates shared
features.  A projection aligns dimensions but not semantic neighborhoods, and
cached targets cannot follow a trainable encoder.

These choices require distinct evidence.  Matched no-condition training tests
overall utility; fixed-checkpoint zero and shuffled controls test route use and
content use; privileged semantic and spatial targets separate predictor error
from interface capacity; independent training seeds measure optimization
robustness; and a mature public checkpoint bounds recovery from weak
fine-tuning.  Simulator seeds estimate rollout variation for one checkpoint
and cannot replace training seeds.  LaWAM and LIBERO are supporting diagnostics
because the former lacks a clean VLA-from-base comparison and the latter is
near saturation for the studied policies.
