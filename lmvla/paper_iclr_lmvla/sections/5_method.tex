\section{Experimental Design}\label{sec:design}

\paragraph{Benchmark and pairing.}
The main experiment uses six RoboTwin 2.0 tasks spanning contact, ranking,
handover, and two- or three-stage stacking.  Each accepted checkpoint is
evaluated on four simulator seeds and 50 frozen scenes per task--seed cell
(1,200 episodes).  Contrasts pair identical scene and episode keys.  Training
seed is the top-level uncertainty unit in a hierarchical bootstrap; paired
episodes are resampled within each equally weighted task.  We report every task
and require the 95\% interval for the macro difference to exclude zero.  A
task-safe claim additionally permits no seed--task regression below -5 points.

\paragraph{Matched policy comparison.}
The corrected $\pi_{0.5}$ recipe uses absolute actions, mean/std normalization,
batch 16, 50,000 updates, and the published optimizer schedule.  At training
seeds 1000--1002 we compare A0 (no milestone), A2-Abs (an offline absolute
milestone token), and A3 (the current-encoder MINT-VLA token).  All nine final
step-49,999 checkpoints pass source, initialization, dataset, normalization,
optimizer, and evaluator-manifest audits.  This matrix is the primary utility
test; earlier joint-delta, batch-64, 20k-update runs are exploratory only.

\paragraph{Fixed-checkpoint content controls.}
To ask whether action depends on the predicted future rather than an extra
route or correlated token, we replace the correct condition with current,
zero, feature-permuted, cross-task, and within-task content while holding the
checkpoint and scenes fixed.  A separate three-seed adapter panel compares
normal inference with shuffled actions, a zeroed route, and masked actions,
with Holm correction over the three preregistered mechanism contrasts.

\paragraph{Privileged upper bounds.}
Three controls remove distinct possible bottlenecks before training further
predictors: same-scene oracle stage transitions, explicit correct-next-subtask
prompts on a frozen public policy, and a ground-truth $4\!\times\!4$ spatial
milestone in a two-task offline action probe.  Each has a frozen no-condition
or current-condition baseline and a content-shuffled control where applicable.
Failure stops the corresponding tracker, semantic predictor, or spatial
predictor expansion.

\paragraph{Policy-preserving predictive branch.}
This independently designed branch leaves inherited $\pi_{0.5}$ visual-language
and action paths intact, stops predictive gradients at inherited features, and
routes a zero-initialized action-conditioned adapter only into the action
expert.  P1 screens seed 1000 against a current-source A0 and route/action
controls.  P2 trains candidate seeds 1001 and 1002 against the fixed seed-1000
A0.  P3 supplies the decisive denominator by independently training matched A0
seeds 1001 and 1002; only the three seed-matched comparison can support a
replicated method claim.  P4 tests content, route, and action-conditioning use,
and P5 compares the candidates with the exact public checkpoint.  The adapter
cost is measured by parameter count, compiled cost, memory, direct inference,
WebSocket latency, and throughput.

\paragraph{Predeclared stopping.}
Recurrence-aligned auxiliary training and outcome-calibrated demonstration
weighting are evaluated as separate extensions.  A one-seed directional screen
may authorize replication but cannot establish a method effect.  New task
panels, a second VLA, or component-factorization training are launched only if
their upstream utility gates pass.  Complete configurations, intervention
identities, multiplicity families, and audit hashes are in
Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix}.
