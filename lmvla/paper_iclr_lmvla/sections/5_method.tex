\section{Experimental Design}\label{sec:design}

\paragraph{Benchmark and paired scenes.}
We use six RoboTwin 2.0 tasks: Hammer, ranking by colour, ranking by size,
Handover, Stack-2, and Stack-3.  Each complete policy condition contains four
evaluation seeds and 50 accepted frozen scenes per task--seed cell, or 1,200
episodes.  Fixed-checkpoint contrasts use identical checkpoint, scene, episode,
observation, action bridge, and budget.  Training comparisons pair episode keys
within matched training seeds.  Partial cells, smoke tests, intermediate
checkpoints, and failed executions never enter a claim-bearing result.

\paragraph{Released-checkpoint content panel.}
TG1A freezes the released LaWAM RoboTwin checkpoint before evaluation.  Normal
inference is compared with three prespecified interventions: a within-task,
different-episode shuffle of the captured normal future grid; a learned null
grid; and persistence of the current visual grid.  Each condition contains the
same 1,200 scenes.  Normal--shuffled identifies use of the particular predicted
content; normal--null tests route necessity; normal--persistence tests whether
the route supplies more than a repeated current state.  Passing the latter two
does not substitute for the shuffled comparison.

\paragraph{Temporal-cadence diagnostic.}
TG1B uses one matched training-seed pair from the historical local all-six-task
setup: a local-WM checkpoint and a future-off checkpoint.  Each is evaluated
while executing either 36 or 50 actions per policy query.  The prespecified
difference-in-differences is
\begin{equation}
 (S_{\mathrm{local},50}-S_{\mathrm{local},36})-
 (S_{\mathrm{off},50}-S_{\mathrm{off},36}).
\end{equation}
This panel can detect local-WM-specific cadence sensitivity for these
checkpoints.  It cannot establish correct-content use or generalize over
training seeds.

\paragraph{Matched MINT-VLA comparison.}
The corrected $\pi_{0.5}$ matrix trains no hint, an offline absolute milestone
token, and MINT-VLA from the same official initialization at training seeds
1000--1002.  All arms use absolute actions, mean/std normalization, batch 16,
50,000 updates, the published optimizer schedule, and the fixed final
step-49,999 checkpoint.  Each of the nine checkpoints passes source,
initialization, dataset, normalization, optimizer, payload, and evaluator
audits.  Fixed-checkpoint current, zero, permuted, within-task, and cross-task
replacements separately test whether correct milestone content is used.

\paragraph{PredictiveActionAdapter gates.}
P0 tests held-out future-grid cosine under normal, temporally reversed, and
masked actions.  P3 is the decisive control comparison: independently trained
candidate and A0 policies are paired at seeds 1000--1002.  P4 holds each
candidate checkpoint fixed and compares normal inference with reversed action
content, a zero route, and masked actions.  The three P4 pooled paired tests
form one Holm family.  We additionally report the exact public-$\pi_{0.5}$
reference and matched parameter, latency, throughput, and memory measurements,
but neither replaces P3 or P4.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and safety gates.}
For fixed-checkpoint panels, hierarchical bootstrap samples tasks, evaluation
seeds within task, and paired episodes.  For replicated training comparisons,
training seed is the highest resampling unit and paired episodes are nested
within each equally weighted task.  All intervals use 20,000 frozen draws.  A
positive claim requires the hierarchical 95\% interval lower endpoint above
zero and, when prespecified, Holm-adjusted paired $p<0.05$.  A task-safe claim
also requires no training-seed--task regression worse than five percentage
points.  We report every task effect rather than allowing a macro average to
hide a regression.

\paragraph{Source decomposition and stopping.}
TG4 freezes six matched LaWAM arms---clean base, future-off, auxiliary-only,
conditioning-only, parameter-matched null, and full---at training seeds
1100--1102.  Its 18 final checkpoints must pass initialization, data-order,
optimizer, dataset, and non-arm equality checks before any evaluation.  The
seven prespecified contrasts isolate pretraining, auxiliary shaping,
conditioning, the full package, and interaction.  TG2 and its recovery were
rejected before evaluation because their training seeds did not induce
independent rank data orders; their checkpoints provide no policy comparison.
No rejected branch is reopened or tuned against its outcomes.
