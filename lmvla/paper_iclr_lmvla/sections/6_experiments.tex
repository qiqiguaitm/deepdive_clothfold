\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Corrected training rejects the milestone interfaces}
The published $\pi_{0.5}$ checkpoint reaches 78.17\% on our frozen six-task,
four-seed RoboTwin evaluator (1,200 episodes), showing that the bridge supports
a mature policy.  It is a calibration rather than a matched baseline because
its training mixture differs.  We therefore train A0 (no hint), A2-Abs
(offline absolute milestone), and A3 (MINT-VLA) from the same official
initialization under the corrected public recipe at seeds 1000--1002.

Across 10,800 rollouts, A0 reaches 79.39\%, A2-Abs 75.44\%, and A3 70.81\%.
The matched hierarchical differences are $-3.94$ points for A2-Abs (95\% CI
[$-6.58$,$-0.97$]) and $-8.58$ for A3
([$-11.47$,$-5.75$]).  A3 regresses on all six tasks; A2-Abs improves only
Stack-2 by 0.50 points.  Thus the internally matched but weak legacy pilot,
where every conditioned arm improved over A0, does not survive the corrected
training protocol.  Full seed-by-task success rates and the legacy matrix are
reported in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix}.

\subsection{Prediction quality and privileged targets do not imply action utility}
The predictor is nontrivial: over two task-stratified held-out folds (480
episodes), latent cosine is 0.8134 versus 0.7479 for persistence, with 46.9\%
top-1 and 80.8\% top-5 milestone retrieval.  At fixed A2 and A3 checkpoints,
however, correct predictions do not outperform current, zero, feature-permuted,
cross-task, or within-task replacements on the common 518 scenes; all pooled
Holm-adjusted $p$-values equal 1.  Nine six-task LaWAM comparisons give the
same null result, with one corrected task-level difference and it is adverse.

Three privileged controls further isolate the failure.  First, oracle stage
transitions change success by only $+0.53$ points over matched A0 across three
training seeds (95\% CI [$-2.06$,$+3.00$]).  Second, a correct next-subtask
prompt reaches 77.08\% on a fixed public policy, below no subtask at 80.83\%
and statistically indistinguishable from shuffled semantics; Handover regresses
by 25 points.  Third, a ground-truth $4\!\times\!4$ milestone grid does not beat
both no-goal and current-image controls in the two-task offline action probe and
significantly worsens Hammer endpoint error.  These tests remove predictor
error, semantic ambiguity, or global pooling in turn, but none establishes that
the future condition is action-sufficient.

\subsection{A policy-preserving adapter also fails independent replication}
A separately scoped predictive adapter preserves inherited visual-language
and action paths and routes a zero-initialized, action-conditioned branch only
to the action expert.  Its seed-1000 screen improves over matched A0 by 13.42
points without a task regression.  Replicating only candidate training against
that fixed A0 also appears positive (+11.61 points; 95\% CI
[$+8.31$,$+14.67$]), but this denominator omits baseline-training variation.

P3 independently trains the missing A0 seeds.  The matched effects become
+13.42, -5.50, and -2.08 points (Table~\ref{tab:predictive-p3}); their mean is
+1.94 with hierarchical 95\% CI [$-5.78$,$+12.75$].  The primary gate fails,
as does task safety.  An exact-paired mature public reference likewise gives
only $+2.44$ points ([$-0.72$,$+5.39$]).  Across candidate seeds, normal minus
shuffled actions is $+0.53$ points ([$-2.14$,$+3.08$]); zero-route and
masked-action contrasts also cross zero after Holm correction.  The fixed-A0
screen therefore establishes neither an independently replicated method effect
nor use of correct predicted content.

The adapter adds 16.82 million parameters (0.50\%).  Direct-model latency
rises by 0.93\,ms (1.63\%), WebSocket round-trip latency by 2.76\,ms (2.48\%),
and matched four-GPU peak training memory by 18\,MiB (0.027\%).  These modest
costs do not rescue the rejected utility and mechanism gates.

\subsection{Additional extensions remain negative}
Adding recurrence-derived CRAVE targets to the adapter lowers seed-1000 success
to 62.92\%, 6.08 points below A0.  Terminal-outcome demonstration weighting
passes a one-seed directional screen, but across three seeds its mean gains are
only +2.81 points over ordinary training (95\% CI [$-0.94$,$+6.58$]) and +3.81
over outcome-free CRAVE weighting ([$-0.25$,$+7.97$]).  Both replication gates
therefore fail.  Correlated recurrence statistics remain useful diagnostics,
not evidence for a reward, value function, or model-predictive controller.

The three independent negative branches close the preregistered selector,
tracker, task-expansion, second-VLA, and component-factorization paths.  We do
not tune new variants against these outcomes.  The evidence is restricted to
the evaluated $\pi_{0.5}$ interfaces and RoboTwin panel; complete controls,
per-seed task tables, audits, and scope qualifications are in the appendix.
