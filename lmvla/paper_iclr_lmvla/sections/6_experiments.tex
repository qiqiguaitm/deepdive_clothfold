\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Calibration: the evaluator supports a mature $\pi_{0.5}$}
The published $\pi_{0.5}$ checkpoint obtains \piRTPublic\% on the same six-task,
four-seed bridge (941/1,200 successes): 93.0 hammer, 96.5 ranking-RGB, 64.0
ranking-size, 53.5 handover, 93.5 Stack-2, and 70.0 Stack-3.  This result is an
evaluator calibration, not a matched baseline, because its training mixture and
recipe differ.  The calibration nevertheless shows that the pilot A0 score is mainly
a policy-training issue rather than a globally broken evaluation bridge.

\subsection{Legacy matched pilot: useful signal, insufficient baseline}
Table~\ref{tab:pi05rt} reports the completed legacy pilot.  Every conditioned
method improves its six-task macro over A0.  A2-Abs gains 13.25 points and A3
gains \piRTAThreeVsZero{} points.  However, the absolute success levels remain
far below the public calibration, each row has one training seed, and A3 exceeds
A2-Abs by only \piRTAThreeVsAbs{} points.  We therefore use this result to choose
the confirmatory arms, not as the final MINT-VLA performance claim.

\subsection{Both milestone-conditioned arms regress across corrected training seeds}
All nine A0/A2-Abs/A3 checkpoints pass the frozen checkpoint, configuration,
normalization, dataset, 24-cell scene-manifest, launch-provenance, and
source-hash audits.  Across training seeds 1000--1002, A0 reaches 79.39\%,
A2-Abs 75.44\%, and A3 70.81\% (Table~\ref{tab:confirmatory}).  Relative to its
matched A0 seed, A2-Abs changes success by $-5.50$, $-5.33$, and $-1.00$
points; A3 changes it by $-8.58$, $-11.08$, and $-6.08$ points.  The paired
hierarchical mean differences are $-3.94$ points for A2-Abs (95\% CI
[$-6.58$,$-0.97$]) and $-8.58$ points for A3
([$-11.47$,$-5.75$]).  Thus neither conclusion is controlled by one training
seed.  At the task-mean level, A2-Abs improves only Stack-2 by 0.50 points and
A3 regresses on all six tasks.

\subsection{Correct future content does not improve the tested one-token interface}
The predictor contains nontrivial future information: across two held-out
task-stratified folds (480 episodes), latent cosine is 0.8134 versus 0.7479 for
persistence, and milestone retrieval reaches 46.9\% top-1 and 80.8\% top-5.
Prediction quality nevertheless does not imply control utility.  At fixed
$\pi_{0.5}$ A2 and A3 checkpoints, correct predictions do not outperform
current, zero, feature-permuted, cross-task, or within-task replacements on the
common 518-scene intersection (Table~\ref{tab:pi05content}); all pooled
Holm-adjusted $p$-values are 1.

Two supporting LaWAM diagnostics reach the same boundary.  A
state-dependent retrieval milestone changes pooled success by only +0.34
points over 1,161 paired episodes ($p{=}0.738$).  Across Absolute, Residual,
and Residual+stop-gradient, correct hints fail to beat zero, cross-task, or
within-task controls in all nine six-task pooled comparisons (1,200 paired
episodes each; all Holm-adjusted $p{=}1$).  The only corrected task-level
difference is adverse: Residual+stop-gradient obtains 72.5\% on Handover with
the correct hint and 82.5\% with a same-task foreign-instance hint (difference
$-10.0$ points; Holm-adjusted $p{=}0.0337$).  Together, the $\pi_{0.5}$ and
LaWAM results rule out a future-content claim for these one-token interfaces;
they do not establish that every world-model interface is ineffective.

\subsection{Oracle stage transitions do not establish replicated control utility}
All three oracle-transition checkpoints and their evaluations pass the frozen
checkpoint, provenance, and 24-cell protocol audits.  The arm reaches 77.75\%,
81.00\%, and 81.00\% at training seeds 1000--1002, compared with 75.50\%,
80.00\%, and 82.67\% for matched A0 (Table~\ref{tab:mt1oracle}).  The resulting
seed effects are $+2.25$, $+1.00$, and $-1.67$ points.  Across 3,600 paired
episodes, the hierarchical mean difference is $+0.53$ points (95\% CI
[$-2.06$,$+3.00$]).  The interval includes zero, so MT1 fails its predeclared
utility gate.

The task means preserve the heterogeneous result.  Handover, Stack-2, and
Stack-3 improve by $+1.67$, $+2.17$, and $+3.17$ points, whereas Hammer,
ranking-RGB, and ranking-size regress by $-1.50$, $-0.83$, and $-1.50$ points.
The oracle route therefore does not provide a reliable control benefit, and
the positive multistage average cannot support a method claim.  Because the
upstream utility gate fails, we do not train the automatic tracker or infer
transition-content use from the seed-1000 controls.

\subsection{Explicit next-subtask semantics do not restore control utility}
We next replace the visual future condition with an explicit semantic upper
bound.  A fixed public $\pi_{0.5}$ checkpoint receives one of five prompts on
the same 240 scenes: no subtask, a generic stage identifier, the current
subtask, the correct next subtask, or a within-task shuffled next subtask.  The
correct next-subtask prompt reaches 77.08\%, below no subtask at 80.83\% and
within-task shuffled semantics at 77.50\%.  Its paired difference from no
subtask is $-3.75$ points (95\% CI [$-10.42$,$+2.92$]); its difference from
shuffled semantics is $-0.42$ points ([$-7.08$,$+6.25$]).  Although the correct
next subtask improves Stack-3 by 12.5 points, it regresses Handover by 25.0
points and Hammer by 7.5 points.  The predeclared confidence-interval,
content-control, and task-safety gates all fail, so we do not train a semantic
predictor.  This screen bounds prompting an unchanged pretrained policy; it
does not test a learned latent or token adapter trained to use subtask labels.

\subsection{Preserving privileged spatial topology does not recover action utility}
The privileged $4\!\times\!4$ probe tests whether global pooling is the sole
bottleneck before training a spatial predictor.  On Stack-3, privileged
endpoint L2 is 0.3378 versus 0.3274 for no goal and 0.3580 for current-image
patches, so it does not beat both controls.  On Hammer, privileged error is
0.3080 versus 0.2355 and 0.2403.  The paired episode bootstrap estimates a
privileged-minus-no-goal Hammer difference of +0.0725 (95\% CI
[+0.0391,+0.1110]); lower is better.  The prespecified gate therefore fails,
and we stop the predicted spatial expansion.  Because this is a 1,000-update,
two-task offline action endpoint probe rather than closed-loop evaluation, it
isolates the failed interface hypothesis without ruling out action-conditioned
subgoals that encode path, contact, assignment, or timing.

\subsection{Replicated negative utility closes the planned scope expansions}
The three independent training seeds measure optimization variation rather
than only rollout variation.  The ordered-construction hypothesis from the
legacy pilot does not survive the corrected matrix: A3 loses 11.67 points on
Stack-3 and 2.83 points on Stack-2 relative to A0, averaged across training
seeds.  A2-Abs gains only 0.50 points on Stack-2 while losing on the other five
tasks.  Because the preregistered positive-utility gate fails, we do not tune
selectors, add a mature-initialization transfer, or broaden the task panel
against this result.  Exact parameter counts, predictor FLOPs, paired memory,
and latency are reported in the appendix.

\subsection{The evidence is restricted to the tested $\pi_{0.5}$ interfaces}
The current RoboTwin experiment instantiates MINT-VLA only on $\pi_{0.5}$.  It
tests the one-token prefix implementation, not its use with another VLA
architecture.  We therefore make no multi-architecture claim and close the
planned second-VLA expansion after the primary positive-utility gate fails.

\subsection{Supporting boundary diagnostics}
LaWAM interventions show why Q2 is necessary: correct hints are not reliably
better than zero, instance-shuffled, or cross-task hints, and the former ``no-WM'' arm
inherits WM-aware pretraining and current DINO conditioning.  It is therefore
named \emph{LaWAM-init/Future-off} and used only to diagnose downstream
integration.  LIBERO is similarly auxiliary: its near-saturated four-suite
aggregate is neutral to negative under milestone conditioning.  Full tables
and multiplicity-aware analyses belong in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix}; neither
diagnostic substitutes for the completed matched $\pi_{0.5}$ matrix.
